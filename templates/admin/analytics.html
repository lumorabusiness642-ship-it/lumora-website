Privacy

What LUMORA can and cannot collect

The system can record page visits, project/portfolio views, WhatsApp/email clicks, referrer, browser user-agent, IP address, time, and an anonymous visitor ID. It cannot automatically discover a visitor's name, email address or phone number from a normal browser visit. Those contact details are only available when the visitor voluntarily submits the contact form (or uses an external service such as WhatsApp/email, where that service handles the conversation).
